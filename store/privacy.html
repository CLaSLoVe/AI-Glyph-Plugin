AI Glyph privacy policy

21 October 2026

AI Glyph redraws the words on a page into the kind of lettering you see on an early AI-generated shop sign. From a distance it is still the page. Up close, some of the characters are not real.

Text on the page

To draw them, the extension reads the visible text on the page, on your device, in the open tab. That text is not saved or uploaded. Inputs, password fields, and other editable areas are skipped. Cookies, account details, payment details, browsing history, and the list of sites you visit are not collected.

Settings

It stores two settings: on or off, and the intensity (5 to 100), using chrome.storage.sync. If Chrome Sync is on, they sync to your Google account. The developer does not receive them.

Network

The extension does not contact any server, and it does not load code from the network.

Sharing

The developer does not sell or transfer this data. Page text does not leave the browser.

Contact

The support email is on the Chrome Web Store listing.

AI Glyph 隐私政策

2026 年 10 月 21 日

AI Glyph 把网页上的字画成早期 AI 生成图里那种招牌字。远远看还是原来的页面，凑近才发现，有些字并不存在。

页面上的文字

要画这些字，扩展会在你自己的设备上、当前标签页里，读取页面上看得见的文字。这些文字不保存，也不上传。输入框、密码框和可编辑的地方会跳过。Cookie、账号、支付信息、浏览记录，以及你打开过的网站，都不读取。

设置

保存的设置只有两个：开或关，还有强度（5 到 100）。用的是 chrome.storage.sync。开了 Chrome 同步的话，这两项会同步到你的 Google 账号。开发者收不到。

网络

扩展不连接服务器，也不从网上加载代码。

共享

开发者不出售、不转让这些数据。页面文字不会离开浏览器。

联系

支持邮箱写在 Chrome 网上应用店的商品页上。
